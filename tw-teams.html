TW teams

Teams are grouped by the guild’s rules: offense 10 = keep for offense; graded higher on defense than offense (4+) = a defense team; plus the teams the guild picks for defense. The guild can add its own teams too (tw-custom-teams.json) — they show as added by the guild. Hover (or tap) a member count to see who can field the team; ↗ opens a team on AhnaldT101’s site.
