Welcome to Cloudy Bay

A Star Wars: Galaxy of Heroes guild — friendly, organized, improving all the time.

Cloudy Bay is a SWGoH guild of up to 50 players. We fight the Rise of the Empire Territory Battle, go to war in Territory Wars and run guild raids together, and we plan it all on our Discord. If you’re active and like playing as a team, you’ll find a home here.

This site is the guild’s notice board. It’s built from public game data and refreshed every few hours: our latest results, every member’s contribution, and what we’re farming next. Members can use it to check their own progress and plan their own farming.

What we expect

  • 4M+ Galactic Power to join.
  • 600 raid tickets every day. They fuel our raids — it’s the one daily thing everyone can do.
  • Territory Battle: deploy every phase, do your combat missions, and fill platoons when asked.
  • Territory War: sign up, set your defense and use your attacks.
  • Raids: attack in every raid, even with a small score.
  • Farm with the guild. Check the farming targets in each topic — mandatory ones first.
  • Going away? Tell an officer on Discord. Inactive members without notice are removed.
  • Leaving and coming back is tracked. A third return isn’t accepted.
